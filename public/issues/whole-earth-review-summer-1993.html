Whole Earth Review, Summer 1993

“This issue is not about Whole Earth and its glorious history, nor about What Went Wrong and What Went Right With the Sixties (though I was tempted), nor about The Great Changes 25 Years Have Wrought, nor even about The Next 25 Years. […] But mostly this issue is just a collection of what I found most interesting and available in the winter of 1992-93. The organizers of the issue are the times and my peculiar curiosity responding to the times.”
