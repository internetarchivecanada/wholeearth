Whole Earth Review, Fall 1995

“War is a hard topic for rational thought. That's not to say that nobody reasons about its methods or its history; there are whole industries for that. […] But what seems to be missing, or at least hard to come by, is a rationale for reacting to a war, a structure for valuing it, comparing it to other wars, and deciding — as I think we have a responsibility to decide — whether a war's costs will be balanced by its justifications.”
