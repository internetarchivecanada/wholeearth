Whole Earth Review, Summer 1995

“Everybody from the president to the peanut vendor talks about prosperity. Are we on the path to it? Have we lost it? Will it be there for our children’s children? Prosperity is the main promise of the American Dream. Its absence is called ‘downward mobility.’ But no one seems to know what it is.”
