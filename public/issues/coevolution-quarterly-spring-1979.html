CoEvolution Quarterly, Spring 1979

“You know about radiation. […] Here we will especially be looking at mutagenicity — the chasing of mutations in human genes — primarily expressed as an increase in miscarriages and birth defects, but perhaps in other ways as well. In this regard the science of human genetics, of enormous consequence, is shockingly imprecise.”

Editor: Stewart Brand
