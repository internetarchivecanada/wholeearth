Whole Earth Review, Summer 1989

“I enjoy technology that augments my body — the computer upon which this essay was typed, the backpack that gives me the freedom of the mountains — but I don’t see that technology as replacing the body. There's a saying: ‘If it ain't broke, don’t fix it.’ The body is a near-miraculous organism of unsurpassed versatility, flexibility, and creativity. What machine can begin to replace all that? And why would we want to?”
