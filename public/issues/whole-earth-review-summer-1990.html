Whole Earth Review, Summer 1990

“This issue never set out to have a theme. It began as a serendipitous catch of all that flows through the office, particularly of the many product, book, and catalog reviews piling up on our shelves. Indeed, there are more reviews than usual in this issue. But it is only now, the last week in production, that an alliance among the ideas on these pages has emerged. What vou have here is an issue pointing to the reality of autonomous worlds. […] In short, welcome to the second Creation, the age of Synthetic Realities.”
