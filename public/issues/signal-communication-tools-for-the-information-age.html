Signal: Communication Tools for the Information Age

“This book limits its investigation to all possible tools having information at one end and a grasp at the other. It considers any idea that can amplify the reach of personal communication, any science which can intercept the power of larger circuits to redirect its flow toward individuals, and any art that can nurture data into information, information into knowledge, and knowledge into wisdom.”
