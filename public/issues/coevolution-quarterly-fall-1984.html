CoEvolution Quarterly, Fall 1984

“I’d seen our sun moving down behind the stationary horizon of an apparently fixed world. Though I knew that the earth spun, I did not see or feel it. The world I experienced was passive and immensely unmoving. But a simple shift in interpretation, from the horizon as stationary to the sun as stationary, caused the horizon and my frame of reference to tilt up in front of the sun.”

Editor: Stewart Brand
