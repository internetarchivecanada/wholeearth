CoEvolution Quarterly, Fall 1983

“This account is not the one-eyed view of exposé journalism. Nor the one-eyed view of an embittered true believer. All life with eyes that isn’t maimed sees with a minimum of two eyes. Joseph Stevenson reports that way on an exceptionally worthy activity that has slipped a bit, not too far for easy repair. This too is love, Mother Teresa. Maintenance and improvement is as inspirational as founding (especially these slipshod days), or what is the Church for?”

Editor: Stewart Brand
