CoEvolution Quarterly, Summer 1979

“Plant a tree — is there any more certain an undubious good? Good for the soil, good for the watershed, good for the atmosphere (trees eat CO2), good for the shade, good for the fruit, the firewood, the lumber, good for the aesthetics […], good for the continuity, good for climbing and tree houses, a cheap, easy, enjoyable investment with non-stop rewards.”

Editor: Stewart Brand
