About

Whole Earth People is an index of the people who wrote, edited and produced the Whole Earth publications: the Whole Earth Catalog and its supplements (1968–1998), CoEvolution Quarterly (1974–1985), the Whole Earth Software Catalog and Review (1984–1985), Whole Earth Review and Whole Earth (1985–2002), and the books published alongside them. It lists every person it could find, and for each one, every issue they appear in and what they did there.

Where the names come from

  • Tables of contents. The Whole Earth Index transcribed the contents of 116 of its 147 issues. Entries written “Title by Name” give the writers; “edited by”, “interview with”, “photographs by” and the like give other roles. Most catalog reviews have no byline in the contents, so they are not counted here.
  • Signed reviews. Whole Earth reviews end with the reviewer’s name after a dash (“—J. Baldwin”). Those signatures were counted in the OCR text of every page. A name is kept if it is signed at least three times, or twice and also credited another way, which drops most OCR misreadings and one-off headings; a few names are quotations credited to their author rather than reviews. The earliest catalogs signed with initials (“—SB”), which are not counted.
  • Editors. The Whole Earth Index names the editor of 59 issues.
  • Mastheads. The staff box of each issue was found in the Internet Archive’s OCR (optical character recognition) text of the scans, and names were read from the lines that follow a job title (“Typesetting”, “Paste-up”, “Subscriptions & Back Issues”…). Staff boxes were found in 82 of the 145 issues with OCR text. OCR misreads letters and mixes up columns, so a name seen on only one masthead is marked with a question mark, and close misspellings of a name seen elsewhere were merged into it. Every masthead credit links to the scanned page.
  • Whole Earth Redux. The authors of the 22 essays published so far at redux.wholeearth.info, linked to the issue each essay is about.

A person who appears under more than one spelling (“Patty Phelan” and “Patricia Phelan”, “Evelyn Eldridge” and “Evelyn Eldridge-Diaz”) is merged where the match is clear. Others may remain separate. Corrections are welcome: info@archive.org.

Reading the issues

The issues themselves are on the Whole Earth Index, made by Barry Threw with Gray Area, with design and development by Jon Gacnik and Mindy Seu. The scans are in the Internet Archive’s Whole Earth collection, digitized from copies donated by Fred Collopy, Kevin Kelly, Danica Remy, Howard Rheingold and the Long Now Foundation. This site links to both and copies none of the publications’ text. The “Whole Earth” wordmark in the header is the Whole Earth Catalog’s, taken from the logo the Whole Earth Index uses. The typefaces are the Whole Earth Index’s versions of Latin Modern (GUST Font License).

Stewart Brand on the people behind the publications, and their photographs: wholeearth50th.com.

Accessibility

The site is plain HTML that works without JavaScript; the name filter on the People page is the only script. It is meant to meet WCAG 2.1 AA (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). If something does not work for you, please write to info@archive.org.
